## Article (198 words)

A new AI system is helping doctors find cancer earlier than before. The system looks at medical pictures and finds small problems that human doctors might miss.

Doctor Sarah Lee works at a hospital in New York. She says: "The AI system is like a second pair of eyes. It helps us see things we might not notice."

The system learns from millions of medical pictures. It can find small changes in the body that may mean cancer is starting. Finding cancer early is very important. When doctors find cancer early, patients have a much better chance of getting well.

"The AI does not replace doctors," says Doctor Lee. "It helps us do our job better. We still make the final decision about each patient."

Many hospitals are now using this new system. The government says it wants all hospitals to have AI help by 2028.

Some patients are happy about the new technology. "I'm glad the doctors have better tools now," says one patient. "It makes me feel safer."
